About

I’m Dima, a designer with a background in banking, classifieds, and marketplaces. I’ve worked with companies and brands including Yandex, Raiffeisen Bank, Decathlon, and Leroy Merlin, as well as small teams and startups. My projects span Russia, Cyprus, and the US.

I like small changes that make products easier to use. There’s often a lot of thought and teamwork behind something that looks simple. I stay hands-on while shaping how design teams work.
