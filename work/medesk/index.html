Medesk

Case study

Connecting clinic workflows, patient care and external services

Project & role

Medesk is a B2B HealthTech SaaS platform for clinics and medical teams. During my work, the platform served 1,000 clinics across 35 countries.

I worked as Senior Product Designer from September 2016 to June 2019. My scope covered clinic operations, patient-facing flows, third-party integrations and a design system.

The challenge

The platform had to connect clinic operations, patient interactions and external services such as medical laboratories. Medesk’s 2018–2019 releases describe keeping laboratory orders and results inside the platform, without re-entering patient data.

Approach

I worked with Product, Engineering, Customer Success and Support across clinic and patient workflows, integration interfaces and reusable UI components.

The solution

Clinic and patient workflows

I designed core workflows for clinic teams and patient management across international markets. Alongside the clinic platform, I designed and helped launch an online treatment flow for 10,000 B2C patients, supporting digital care and patient communication.

Connected services inside the platform

I designed integration flows for 10 third-party modules and five major medical laboratories. This work brought external services and laboratory data into connected healthcare workflows.

Outcomes

  • Launched an online treatment flow for 10,000 B2C patients.
  • NPS improved from 37 to 54.
  • Designed integration flows for 10 third-party modules and five major medical laboratories.

Team feedback

Behind the scenes

A design system tied to implementation

I developed a Storybook-based design system, with reusable UI components and interface patterns for the platform’s core workflows and integrations.
