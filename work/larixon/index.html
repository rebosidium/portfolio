Larixon Classifieds

Building a design function across markets, teams and products

Project & role

Larixon Classifieds operates a group of local marketplaces across Cyprus, Mongolia, Tajikistan, Trinidad & Tobago and Jamaica, with around 5M monthly active users. Its brands include Bazaraki, Unegui, Somon, Pin.tt and JaCars.

As Design Lead since August 2025, I lead seven Product Designers and a UX Research Lead across 8+ product verticals. My remit combines Product Design, UX Research, DesignOps and Design Systems for web and app.

  • Larixon — group, brands and markets

The challenge

The design function needed to support multiple brands, countries and product verticals across web and app. My brief was to build shared research, design-system and delivery practices while accommodating the needs of local teams and markets.

Research & process

I established UX Research across Cyprus, Mongolia and Tajikistan, working with a dedicated research lead on cross-country studies.

I introduced AI-assisted workflows for discovery, research synthesis, hypothesis exploration and documentation.

The solution

A shared system for web and app

I built the design system from the ground up, including reusable foundations, governance, Design System Owner roles and a maturity tracker. The system provided shared components for key product flows across web and app.

I implemented Figma Code Connect to link design components with their code counterparts and support design-to-development handover.

DesignOps connected to product delivery

I established OKR-based design reviews and Jira visibility so product teams could see design priorities and ongoing work.

Outcomes

  • Reached 90% component coverage for key flows in the web and app design system.
  • Established a UX Research function across Cyprus, Mongolia and Tajikistan.
  • Put design-system governance, Code Connect and OKR-based design reviews into use.

Behind the scenes

Developing designers

Alongside product and system work, I supported designers through one-on-ones, mentoring, onboarding and individual development plans.
