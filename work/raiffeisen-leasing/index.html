Raiffeisen Leasing

Raiffeisen Leasing serves corporate clients with a €600M portfolio. I led design for its leasing application and management platform for B2B clients and car dealers.

Kind words from colleagues

Context

From 2021 to 2025, I worked across the leasing journey, from the initial application and lease calculation to ongoing contract management. The platform needed to support B2B clients and car dealers with consistent workflows.

My contribution

As Lead Product Designer, I redesigned application and contract management flows and established shared UX patterns for B2B clients and car dealers.

I led designers across product teams, supported hiring, and ran design reviews, one-on-ones, and mentoring sessions. I helped shape the design roadmap and how the teams worked with business stakeholders.

I also developed an AI-assisted system that helped managers compare offers from suppliers and insurers and choose the best options for each leasing deal.

Outcomes

The changes improved conversion across key steps: completing the application form, calculating a lease, and submitting an application.

I also helped launch a new car leasing platform in collaboration with the partner network.
